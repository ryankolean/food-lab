# Laksa Chicken, Rice and Broth

> **Stub.** Not written or tested yet.

## Starting notes

- Three components: a laksa-style coconut broth, coconut-garlic rice, and a spiced marinated chicken breast. Serves 1 as written, so scale up.
- Full quantities in the caption.
- The broth would freeze well on its own; the rice and chicken are made fresh.

## Method

1. Broth: heat the coconut oil, sauté garlic, lemongrass, galangal, ginger and laksa paste until fragrant. Add the coconut milk, chicken broth, fish sauce, tamarind, palm sugar, lime leaf and coriander root. Simmer gently to infuse.
2. Rice: sauté the spring onion, garlic and ginger in coconut oil for a minute. Add the washed rice and salt, mix, then the coconut milk and water. Cook until the liquid has gone and the rice is fluffy.
3. Chicken: mix the tamarind, ginger, curry powder, turmeric, ground coriander, Thai chilli paste, fish sauce, soy sauce, salt and pepper. Reserve a little, coat the chicken with the rest. Fry in coconut oil until golden and caramelised.
4. Greens: in the same pan, cook the bok choy with the reserved marinade and a splash of water until just tender and glossy.
5. Serve the chicken and greens over the rice with the broth ladled around.
