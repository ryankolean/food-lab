# Smoky Chicken Skewers with Garlic Yogurt Marinade

> **Stub.** Not written or tested yet.

## Starting notes

- Yogurt-marinated grilled chicken thigh skewers, finished with a tangy herb oil and served with cauliflower tabbouleh. Serves 3–4.
- The marinade is fully specified. The herb oil — the more interesting half — was comment-gated and is not written down anywhere in the post.
- The marinade itself freezes with the raw chicken, which suits the vacuum-sealed freezer prep routine.
- Ledger entry: [`prospects/instagram.md`](../prospects/instagram.md) — entry 47.

## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient | Notes |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 2 lbs | Chicken thighs | boneless skinless, cut into skewer-sized chunks |
| 2/3 cup | Greek yogurt | full-fat preferred |
| 1 | Lemon | zest and juice |
| 4 | Garlic cloves | grated or finely minced |
| 1.5 tsp | Ground cumin |  |
| 1.5 tsp | Smoked paprika |  |
| 1.5 tsp | Dried oregano |  |
| 1.5 tsp | Aleppo pepper |  |
| 1.5 tsp | Kosher salt |  |
| 1/2 tsp | Black pepper |  |
| 1 1/2 tbsp | Honey |  |
| 2 tbsp | Extra virgin olive oil |  |
| 1 cup | Tangy herb oil | recipe not given in the post |

## Method

1. Whisk the yogurt, lemon zest and juice, garlic, cumin, smoked paprika, oregano, Aleppo pepper, salt, pepper, honey and olive oil until smooth.
2. Add the chicken and toss until fully coated. Cover and refrigerate at least 1 hour; longer is better.
3. Thread onto skewers.
4. Grill over medium-high heat 4–5 minutes per side until charred and 165°F internal.
5. Drizzle with the herb oil while hot and serve with cauliflower tabbouleh.
