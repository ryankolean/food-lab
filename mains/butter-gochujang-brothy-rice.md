## Method

1. Marinate the chicken, then coat in seasoned potato starch and fry until crisp.
2. Melt the butter, cook the garlic and gochujang, then add the cream, milk, bouillon and seasonings and simmer to a pourable sauce.
3. Serve the chicken on a bed of medium grain rice with the sauce poured around, topped with green onion and fried shallots.
