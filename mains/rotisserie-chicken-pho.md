# Rotisserie Chicken Pho

> **Stub.** Not written or tested yet.

## Starting notes

- Pho built from a supermarket rotisserie chicken — the meat is stripped for the bowl and the carcass makes the broth in a pressure cooker.
- The caption lists the chicken bouillon and fish sauce twice; treat the amounts below as the single intended quantities and confirm when cooking.
- The broth is the part worth freezing.

## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient | Notes |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | Rotisserie chicken | meat stripped, bones reserved |
| 1 | Onion | large |

| 2 tbsp | Chicken bouillon |  |
| 1.5 tbsp | Fish sauce |  |
| golf ball sized | Rock sugar | or ~2 tbsp sugar |
| ~13 cups | Water |  |
|  | Fresh rice stick noodles |  |
|  | Onion, cilantro, bean sprouts, basil | thinly sliced / to top |
|  | Lime | to finish |

## Method

1. Remove all the meat from the rotisserie chicken and put the bones in the pressure cooker.
2. Add the onion, ginger, pho spice pouch, rock sugar and water.
3. Pressure cook on high for 20 minutes.
4. Quick release, add the chicken bouillon and fish sauce, stir and strain.
5. Serve over fresh rice noodles with the chicken meat.
6. Top with herbs, bean sprouts, sliced onion and a squeeze of lime.
