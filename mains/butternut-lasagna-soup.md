# Butternut Lasagna Soup

> **Stub.** Not written or tested yet.

## Starting notes

- Lasagna soup where the creamy base is roasted butternut squash and jarred roasted red peppers blended with cream, rather than a béchamel.
- Full quantities in the caption.

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 400°F. Spread the cubed squash on a sheet pan, drizzle with olive oil, season and roast about 30 minutes until tender.
2. Blend the roasted squash with the roasted red peppers and heavy cream until smooth. Set aside.
3. Heat olive oil in a large pot. Soften the onion about 3 minutes, add the garlic for another minute.
4. Add the beef and sausage, break up and brown. Stir in the Italian seasoning and tomato.
5. Pour in the squash-pepper sauce and the beef broth. Add the broken lasagna sheets and simmer 10–15 minutes until al dente.
6. Stir in the spinach until wilted, then the mozzarella and parmesan until melted.
7. Serve topped with extra parmesan and a dollop of ricotta.
