## Method

1. Season and sear the steak. Rest, then slice thin.
2. In the same pan, sauté the mushrooms and bok choy until tender but still with bite.
3. Heat broth with coconut aminos, garlic, lime, black pepper and a little sesame oil.
4. Build the bowl: vegetables, steak, raw red onion, scallion and lots of cilantro.
5. Pour the boiling broth over at the end.
