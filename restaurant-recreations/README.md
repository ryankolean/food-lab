# Restaurant Recreations

Dishes eaten out and rebuilt at home. Each one starts with research into what the restaurant actually
serves (menu listing, reviews, social posts) to pin down the flavors, then a home version is written and
tested.

## For each recreation, record

- The restaurant, its location, and when you ate the dish
- What you found in research: menu wording, photos, reviews that mention ingredients
- What you're guessing and what you know for sure
- A side-by-side comparison with your memory of the original, if you can get it again
