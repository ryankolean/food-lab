# Mallow London Tiramisu Brioche French Toast

> **Stub.** Not written or tested yet.

## Starting notes

- Original dish: Mallow, Borough Market, London — a brunch menu regular.
- The restaurant posted the full recipe as a comment on the reel; quantities below are theirs.
- Entirely dairy-free: plant cream throughout, with cocoa butter doing the setting work.
- The brioche is dipped in plain plant cream, not a custard — no egg in the soak.
- Ledger entry: [`prospects/instagram.md`](../prospects/instagram.md) — entry 46.

## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient | Notes |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 90 ml | Plant cream | tiramisu cream |
| 9 g | Caster sugar | tiramisu cream |
| 4 g | Cocoa butter | tiramisu cream |
| 1 g | Vanilla extract with seed | tiramisu cream |
| 1 g | Vanilla essence | tiramisu cream |
| pinch | Salt | tiramisu cream |
| 40 ml | Plant cream | espresso anglaise |
| 4 g | Caster sugar | espresso anglaise |
| 2 g | Cocoa butter | espresso anglaise |
| 1/2 tsp | Vanilla extract with seed | espresso anglaise |
| pinch | Salt | espresso anglaise |
| 2 tsp | Espresso | espresso anglaise |
| 1 slice | Brioche | store bought |
| 100 ml | Plant cream | for dipping |
|  | Cocoa powder | to dust |

## Method

1. Tiramisu cream: heat all the ingredients except the plant cream and blend until smooth. Chill completely, then add the cream and whip to soft peaks.
2. Espresso anglaise: heat the ingredients together and blend until smooth. Cool completely.
3. Dip the brioche into plant cream until coated on both sides.
4. Fry in a pan until golden and crisp on both sides.
5. Pipe the tiramisu cream onto the brioche and dust with cocoa powder. Serve the anglaise alongside or poured over.
