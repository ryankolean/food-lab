# Tahini-Halva Upside-Down Banana Bread

> **Stub.** Not written or tested yet.

## Starting notes

- Banana bread baked as a 9-inch upside-down cake: a caramel and sliced banana layer on the bottom, tahini, dark chocolate and halva in the batter.
- Full quantities and method were given in the caption.
- Olive oil rather than butter in the batter, which should help it keep and freeze well.
- Ledger entry: [`prospects/instagram.md`](../prospects/instagram.md) — entry 37.

## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient | Notes |

| 2 cups | All-purpose flour |  |

| 1/2 tsp | Ground cinnamon |  |
| 1/2 tsp | Ground nutmeg |  |
| 1 cup | Dark chocolate | chips or chopped |
| 1/2 cup | Halva | crumbled |

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Grease a 9-inch square pan and line with parchment. Place the sliced bananas cut side down on the parchment.
2. Combine the sugar and water in a saucepan over medium-high heat. Cook without stirring, shaking the pan if needed, to an amber caramel, about 6 minutes. Pour over the bananas in an even layer.
3. Whisk the brown sugar and mashed bananas to a relatively smooth paste. Whisk in the olive oil, tahini, vanilla and eggs.
4. Add the flour, salt, baking soda, baking powder, cinnamon and nutmeg. Stir until just incorporated, then fold in the chocolate and halva.
5. Pour over the caramel-coated bananas and smooth the top. Bake 45–50 minutes, until golden and cracked with an internal temperature of 190°F.
6. Cool 15 minutes, run a paring knife around the edge, invert onto a plate and discard the parchment. Serve warm.
