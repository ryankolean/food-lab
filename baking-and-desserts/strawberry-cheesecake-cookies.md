# Strawberry Cheesecake Cookies

> **Stub.** Not written or tested yet.

## Starting notes

- Cookies with roasted strawberries, a jam swirl and frozen cheesecake shards folded through.
- Full quantities in both cups and grams. Yield 12 cookies with a size 16 scoop.
- Two prep steps happen well ahead: the cheesecake layer freezes solid before it is cut into shards, and the dough chills overnight.
- Spread the cheesecake mix on parchment, not straight on a tray, or it sticks.
- Ledger entry: [`prospects/instagram.md`](../prospects/instagram.md) — entry 45.

## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient | Notes |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 cup (227 g) | Butter | softened — dough |
| 1 cup (200 g) | White sugar | dough |
| 1/2 cup + 2 tbsp (120 g) | Brown sugar | dough |
| 1 tbsp (15 g) | Milk or cream | dough |
| 1 + 1 yolk | Egg | dough |
| 1 tbsp (15 g) | Vanilla bean paste | dough |
| 1/2 tsp | Cornstarch | dough |
| 2 1/4 cups (284 g) | All-purpose flour | dough |
| 3/4 tsp | Baking soda | dough |
| 1 tsp | Salt | dough |
| 1 pint (~2 cups) | Strawberries | sliced and roasted |
| 3–4 tbsp | Jam | to swirl |
| 8 oz (226 g) | Cream cheese | cheesecake shards |
| 1 tsp | Vanilla bean paste | cheesecake shards |
| 1 tsp | Cornstarch | cheesecake shards |

## Method

1. Mix the cheesecake shard ingredients, spread in a thin layer on parchment and freeze completely. Cut into shards.
2. Bake the sliced strawberries at 250°F (120°C) for 15 minutes and cool.
3. Cream the butter and sugars. Mix in the egg, yolk, vanilla and milk.
4. Stir in the dry ingredients until a soft dough forms.
5. Fold in the strawberries and cheesecake chunks, then swirl in the jam.
6. Scoop 12 balls, cover and chill overnight.
7. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 10–12 minutes.
8. Shape with a cutter while still warm.
