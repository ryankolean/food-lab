# No-Bake S'mores Cereal Mix

> **Stub.** Not written or tested yet.

## Starting notes

- Muddy-buddy style s'mores mix — cereal coated in chocolate, then tumbled in powdered sugar and graham crumbs. No oven.
- The caption lists 4 cups of golden grahams 'divided' but only uses 3 in the method. Work out where the last cup goes, or reduce it.

## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient | Notes |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 1/2 cup | Chocolate chips | for melting |
| 2 tsp | Coconut oil |  |
| 4 cups | Golden grahams | divided — 3 cups used in the method |
| 3 cups | Rice chex |  |
| 1 1/4 cups | Powdered sugar |  |
| 3/4 cup | Graham cracker crumbs |  |
| 1 1/4 cup | Mini marshmallows |  |
| 1 cup | Milk chocolate chips | stirred in at the end |

## Method

1. Melt the chocolate chips with the coconut oil in a microwave-safe bowl.
2. Put 3 cups golden grahams and 3 cups rice chex in a zip-loc bag. Pour the chocolate over, seal and shake until coated.
3. Add the powdered sugar and graham cracker crumbs to the bag and shake until combined.
4. Stir through the mini marshmallows and milk chocolate chips.
