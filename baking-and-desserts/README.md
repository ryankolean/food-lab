## For each recipe, record

- Pan size and exact bake time and temperature, plus the internal temperature if you took one
- What the dough or batter looked like at each stage — these are the notes that make a bake repeatable
- Whether the dough portions freeze, and how they bake from frozen
- Any scaling you did, and whether the recipe survived it
