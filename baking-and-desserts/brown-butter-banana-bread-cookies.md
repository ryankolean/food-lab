# Brown Butter Banana Bread Cookies

| heaping 2/3 cup | Chocolate chips |  |

## Method

1. Melt the butter in a small saucepan over medium heat, stirring continuously until bubbling and browned, 5–8 minutes. Cool 20 minutes.
2. Whisk the cooled brown butter, maple syrup and banana until smooth. Whisk in the egg and vanilla.
3. Fold in the almond flour, baking soda and salt and stir about a minute until the flour starts to absorb the liquid. The dough should be wet, like banana bread batter.
4. Chill 15 minutes while the oven preheats. Do not skip this.
5. Preheat to 375°F and line a large baking sheet with parchment.
6. Fold in the chocolate chips and scoop 12 cookies onto the sheet.
7. Bake 9–12 minutes until the outsides are golden and the centres still soft.
8. Cool 15 minutes before moving — they are too soft to handle before that.
