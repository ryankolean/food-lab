# XL Buttermilk Banana Bread

> **Stub.** Not written or tested yet.

## Starting notes

- Enormous buttermilk and molasses banana bread baked in XL loaf pans, brushed with maple syrup straight out of the oven.
- **The quantities below were read off the burned-in subtitles, not a written recipe.** One ingredient at 20 g was not legible. Verify everything before scaling down — and it needs scaling down, this is several loaves' worth.
- Creator's notes: bake took roughly 2 h 30 min; start checking at 90 minutes and go until the interior hits 200°F. They would add more salt next time to push the banana flavour.
- Freezes well — wrap and thaw at room temperature. Reportedly moister after freezing.

## Method

1. Beat the butter with the light brown sugar.
2. Add the mashed bananas — the mixture will look split, which is expected.
3. Add the eggs one at a time.
4. Combine the flour and baking soda separately, then fold the wet into the dry.
5. Add the buttermilk and mix until combined.
6. Divide between XL loaf pans and bake at 325°F. Check from 90 minutes; done at 200°F internal.
7. Brush with maple syrup as soon as they come out of the oven.
