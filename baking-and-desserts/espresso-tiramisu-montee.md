| 370 g | White chocolate |  |
| 6 g | Gelatin | bloomed |
| 270 ml | Heavy cream | hot |
| 550 ml | Heavy cream | cold |
| 169 g | Mascarpone |  |
| 80 g | Espresso | or more to taste |
| 30 ml | Marsala |  |
| 50 ml | Glucose |  |

## Method

1. Bloom the gelatin.
2. Heat the smaller quantity of cream with the glucose and pour over the white chocolate; add the gelatin and emulsify.
3. Add the espresso, Marsala and mascarpone.
4. Add the cold cream, blend smooth and refrigerate overnight.
5. Whip to piping consistency before use.
