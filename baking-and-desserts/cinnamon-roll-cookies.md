# Cinnamon Roll Cookies

> **Stub.** Not written or tested yet.

## Starting notes

- Brown butter cookie dough marbled with a cinnamon-butter swirl, so it eats like a cinnamon roll.
- Full recipe was given in French; quantities below are as published. About 10 cookies of 80–90 g.
- Two tricks worth keeping: mix the cinnamon paste in very roughly for the marble, and bake inside 9–10 cm tart rings if you want them even.
- Portioned dough balls freeze — they go into the freezer for 30–45 minutes before baking anyway.
- Ledger entry: [`prospects/instagram.md`](../prospects/instagram.md) — entry 7.

## Method

1. Mix the softened butter with the vergeoise blonde, cinnamon and salt. Set aside.
2. Brown the 120 g of butter and let it cool to warm.
3. Mix the brown butter with the sugars, then add the egg.
4. Add the flour, bicarbonate, baking powder and salt. Mix without overworking, then fold in the chocolate.
5. Add the cinnamon mixture and mix very roughly, to marble it.
6. Shape 80–90 g balls and freeze 30–45 minutes.
7. Bake at 165°C fan for 13–15 minutes — edges golden, centres still soft.
8. Cool 10 minutes on the tray and add a small pinch of fleur de sel.
