### 1. Brothy steak bowl: @wellbybell
<https://www.instagram.com/reel/DdkHhs3N1KY/>

- **Caption summary:** "Steak bowl obsession" — seared steak sliced thin over sautéed mushrooms and bok
  choy, hot broth poured over at the end. Deliberately not soupy: mostly steak and vegetables with just
  enough broth to bring it together.
- **Ingredients (as shown):** lean steak · bok choy · mushrooms · thinly sliced red onion · scallions ·
  cilantro · broth · coconut aminos or tamari · fresh garlic · lime · black pepper · tiny drizzle of
  sesame oil. No quantities given.
- **Method:** Season and sear the steak, rest, slice thin. Sauté mushrooms and bok choy in the same pan
  until tender with bite. Heat broth with coconut aminos, garlic, lime, black pepper and a little sesame
  oil. Build the bowl with vegetables, steak, red onion, scallion and lots of cilantro; pour the boiling
  broth over at the end.
- **On-screen notes:** Frames also show rice in the bowl (the caption omits it). Broth is mixed cold in a
  glass bowl, then poured over "when it is super boiling hot".
- **Overlap:** none.
- **Notes:** The broth is essentially a thin pan sauce — could be worth testing as a freezer concentrate.

### 2. XL buttermilk banana bread: @butterlucknxttime
<https://www.instagram.com/reel/DdhGNDpRDen/>

- **Caption summary:** Credits @theselena_v's banana bread recipe. Makes an enormous amount; worth buying

  straight out of the oven. Extras freeze and thaw at room temperature (reportedly moister after freezing).
- **Ingredients (as shown):** From on-screen narration only: 595 g unsalted butter · 800 g light brown

- **On-screen notes:** Bake at 325°F. Creator says roughly 2 h 30 min; start checking at 90 min and bake
  until the interior reaches 200°F. Considering more salt next time.
- **Overlap:** none. Near-duplicate theme with #37 and #44 (banana bread).
- **Notes:** Quantities read off burned-in subtitles, not a written recipe — verify before scaling down.

### 7. Cinnamon roll cookies: @gallymini_
<https://www.instagram.com/reel/DclyzMZNHF2/>

- **Caption summary:** French-language full recipe. Makes about 10 cookies of 80–90 g.
- **Ingredients (as shown):** *Cinnamon swirl* — 120 g vergeoise blonde (soft brown sugar) · 90 g soft
  unsalted butter · 10 g cinnamon · 1 g fleur de sel. *Cookie dough* — 120 g unsalted butter · 100 g white
  sugar · 60 g vergeoise brune · 1 egg · 220 g flour · 2 g bicarbonate of soda · 2 g baking powder · fleur
  de sel · 170 g milk chocolate.
- **Method:** Mix soft butter with brown sugar, cinnamon, salt; reserve. Brown the 120 g butter, cool.
  Mix brown butter with sugars, add egg, then dry ingredients; do not overwork. Fold in chocolate. Add the
  cinnamon mixture and mix very roughly for a marbled effect. Roll 80–90 g balls, freeze 30–45 min.
- **On-screen notes:** Bake at 165°C fan, 13–15 min — edges golden, centre still soft. Optionally bake in
  9–10 cm tart rings for even shape. Cool 10 min on the tray, finish with fleur de sel.
- **Overlap:** none.

### 12. Tiger skin eggs with runny yolk: @foodmymuse

### 13. Whipped brie with lemon-rosemary curd: @foodmymuse
<https://www.instagram.com/reel/DcH68cbCurQ/>

- **Caption summary:** Season 2 ep. 1 of "Just A Bite" — sweet-tart curd over rich whipped brie. Leftover
  curd keeps a week in the fridge.
- **Ingredients (as shown):** 4 egg yolks · 5 oz cold unsalted butter · 1/3 cup lemon juice · zest of
  1 lemon · 1 rosemary sprig (plus more for frying) · 2/3 cup cane sugar · pinch of salt · 10 oz brie
  (no rind) · 3 oz cream cheese · pinch of flaky salt · crushed pistachio · a tiny drizzle of honey.
- **Method:** Massage rosemary leaves, sugar and lemon zest together to release oils. Add yolks, salt and
