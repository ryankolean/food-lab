# Christmas Cookies: The 24

A Christmas cookie selection capped at 24, one per day like an advent calendar. The rule for getting in:
the specific recipe has to have outside evidence that it is worth making. That means a win in a blind
head-to-head bake-off, a high rating from a lot of people, or a long public reputation. A cookie type
with no standout recipe was left out, however traditional.

Nothing here has been made yet. These are prospects. Each one gets stubbed into
[Baking and Desserts](../baking-and-desserts/) when it's picked up for a test batch.

## How far to trust this (read first)

Researched 2026-11-15 across bake-off sites, recipe sites with public ratings, magazine taste tests and
contest archives.

- **No recipe page or bake-off page could be opened.** The session's network blocked every recipe site,
  so every rating, count and bake-off result below was read from search-result text, not from the page
  itself. Treat the numbers as approximate snapshots and confirm each one when the recipe is stubbed.
- **Ingredients and methods were not read.** That is why these are log entries and not recipes.
- **Sources that could not be searched at all:** Reddit, Allrecipes, NYT Cooking's own rating pages,
  Serious Eats, Bon Appétit and Epicurious. A recipe whose reputation lives mostly on those sites is
  under-represented here.
- Links marked *(link unconfirmed)* were not seen in a search result and are from memory.

Evidence grades used in the table:

- **A**: won or was the named top pick in a blind multi-recipe bake-off, or has a high rating from
  several hundred people or more.
- **B**: a solid rating from a smaller crowd, or a strong reputation without a number behind it.
- **C**: the best recipe found for a type that earns its place, but the evidence for this specific recipe
  is thin. First candidates to swap.

## What the 24 cover

- **Spice (5):** lebkuchen, gingersnaps, soft glazed gingerbread, burnt sugar ginger molasses, gochujang
  caramel
- **Butter and shortbread (5):** Scottish shortbread, salted chocolate chunk shortbread, snowballs,
  decorated cut-outs, Lofthouse style
- **Jam and filled (4):** thumbprints, rugelach, Linzer, rainbow cookies
- **Chocolate and peanut butter (5):** World Peace, crinkles, peppermint bark, blossoms, buckeyes
- **Fruit, nut and citrus (5):** biscotti, melomakarona, macaroons, white chocolate cranberry, lemon
  ricotta

Gluten-free as written, going by the descriptions: lebkuchen (flourless), coconut macaroons, buckeyes.
Check each recipe before relying on that.

## Left out on purpose

- **Chocolate chip and snickerdoodles.** The house versions are already finished:
  [brown butter chocolate chip](../baking-and-desserts/brown-butter-chocolate-chip-cookies.md),
  [snickerdoodle](../baking-and-desserts/snickerdoodle-cookies.md).
- **Spritz.** Needs a press; sticking and spreading dominate the reviews, and ratings ran 3.9 to 4.5.
- **Pinwheels.** Fiddly, and "bland" is the recurring review.
- **Red velvet crinkles and eggnog cookies.** Pretty but bland, and the lowest ratings in the drop-cookie
  group (King Arthur eggnog cookies: 3.9 from 16).
- **Homemade Oreos.** Soften within a day once filled.
- **Magic bars.** Consistently "too sweet", no standout recipe.
- **Springerle, pepparkakor, speculoos.** Special molds or long resting, and no well-rated recipe found.
  Sources themselves point to store-bought for the last two.
- **Stroopwafels and krumkake.** Special irons, one at a time, best only when fresh.
- **Pignoli and florentines.** Pignoli are costly for what they are; no well-rated florentine was found.
