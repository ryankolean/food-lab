# House Olive Brine and Extra Dirty Martini

> **Stub.** Not written or tested yet.

## Starting notes

## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient | Notes |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 0.5 tbsp | Kosher salt | brine |
| 0.5 oz | 15 ml | Vinegar | brine — type not specified |
| 3 oz | 90 ml | Dry vermouth | brine |
| 5 oz | 150 ml | Filtered water | brine |
| 340 g | Castelvetrano olives | brine |
| 1.5 oz | 45 ml | Housemade olive brine | martini |
| 1.5 oz | 45 ml | Gin | martini — your choice |

## Method

1. Combine the salt, vinegar, dry vermouth and filtered water, and brine the olives in it.
2. For the martini, stir the brine and gin over ice.
3. Strain into a frozen glass.
4. Garnish with the marinated olives.
