# Caramelized Onions, Microwave First

> **Stub.** Not written or tested yet.

## Starting notes

- Technique, not a recipe. Onions are about 90% water, and that water is what stops them browning. Microwave the sliced onions first to drive it off, then caramelize in the pan.
- Claim: roughly 15 minutes in the pan instead of the hour-long method, and more evenly browned.
- No times, quantities or power levels were given for the microwave step — that is what needs testing.
- Caramelized onions freeze well, so this is a useful base for the freezer library. Feeds [`sauces/compound-butters/french-onion-butter.md`](../sauces/compound-butters/french-onion-butter.md).
- Ledger entry: [`prospects/instagram.md`](../prospects/instagram.md) — entry 26.

## Method

1. Slice the onions and microwave them to drive off the water. Time and power not specified — test this.
2. Tip them, with their liquid drained, into a hot pan with fat.
3. Caramelize for about 15 minutes, stirring, until evenly browned.
