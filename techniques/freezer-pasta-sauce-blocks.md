# Freezer Pasta Sauce Blocks

> **Stub.** Not written or tested yet.

## Starting notes

- A portioning format, not a recipe: sauces frozen flat in ziplock bags, scored into blocks so one block equals one portion. Blocks stack upright in the freezer door.
- To serve, drop a frozen block straight into a hot pan, let it thaw and loosen, then add cooked pasta.
- This is a direct alternative to the ice cube tray / 1-cup container standard in [`sauces/README.md`](../sauces/README.md) — flat bags thaw fast and the scoring gives exact portions without a mold.
- The sauces named in the post include roasted red pepper and feta, baked vegetable, creamy chicken and mushroom, caramelized onion, a claimed freezer-friendly carbonara, spicy peanut sesame, cherry tomato with mini meatballs and butter chicken. The recipes are in their paid cookbook and were not opened.

## Method

1. Cool the sauce completely.
2. Fill a freezer bag, press flat and push out the air.
3. Score the flat bag into equal blocks with a chopstick or the edge of a bench scraper before it freezes.
4. Freeze flat, then stand the bags upright once solid.
5. To serve, snap off a block and drop it straight into a hot pan. Loosen, then add cooked pasta.
