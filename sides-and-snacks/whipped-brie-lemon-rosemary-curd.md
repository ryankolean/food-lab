# Whipped Brie with Lemon-Rosemary Curd

> **Stub.** Not written or tested yet.

## Starting notes

- Whipped brie and cream cheese under a lemon curd infused with rosemary, topped with pistachio, honey and fried rosemary.
- Full quantities in the caption. There will be leftover curd — it keeps a week in the fridge.
- The rosemary, sugar and lemon zest get massaged together first to release the oils; that is the step doing the work.
- Ledger entry: [`prospects/instagram.md`](../prospects/instagram.md) — entry 13.

## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient | Notes |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 4 | Egg yolks | curd |
| 5 oz | Unsalted butter | cold — curd |
| 1/3 cup | Lemon juice | curd |
| 1 | Lemon | zest — curd |
| 1 sprig | Rosemary | plus more for frying |
| 2/3 cup | Cane sugar | curd |
| pinch | Salt | curd |
| 10 oz | Brie | no rind |
| 3 oz | Cream cheese |  |
| pinch | Flaky salt | to finish |
|  | Pistachio | crushed, to top |
|  | Honey | a tiny drizzle |

## Method

1. Put the rosemary leaves, sugar and lemon zest in a saucepan and massage together to release the oils.
2. Add the egg yolks, a pinch of salt and the lemon juice.
3. Whisk over medium heat until it coats the back of a spoon and hits 175–180°F.
4. Off the heat, whisk in the butter. Strain, cover with plastic wrap touching the surface and chill completely.
5. Process the brie and cream cheese in a food processor until smooth.
6. Fry a few rosemary leaves in olive oil for about 30 seconds until crisp.
7. Pipe the dip onto a plate and top with the curd, pistachios, honey, flaky salt and fried rosemary.
