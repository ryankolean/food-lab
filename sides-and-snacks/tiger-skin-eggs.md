# Tiger Skin Eggs with Runny Yolk

## Method

1. Pierce the eggs. Add to a pot of rolling water, cover and boil 6–6.5 minutes.
2. Move to ice water for 10 minutes.
3. Tap the egg gently all over with a spoon and roll it very gently on a kitchen towel. Return to the water for 10 more minutes.
4. Peel and refrigerate 1 hour.
5. Make shallow cuts all over with a knife.
6. Preheat a pan 2–4 minutes on medium-high, add oil, wait 30 seconds, then fry the eggs 1–2 minutes per side. Remove, along with some of the oil.
7. Sauté the ginger and garlic 1–2 minutes.
8. Mix the soy sauce, dashi, rice vinegar, mirin, sambal, sesame oil and sugar. Add to the pan and cook 3–4 minutes.
9. Whisk the corn starch into the 1/4 cup water and add to the sauce. Thicken over medium-low, 5–6 minutes, whisking often, to cook out the starch.
10. Off the heat, return the eggs and coat. Top with sesame seeds and chives.
