## Method

1. Brine the potatoes in salted water 20 minutes, then boil until fork-tender.
2. Smash on a parchment-lined sheet and drizzle with oil and minced garlic.
3. Roast at 400°F for 25–30 minutes, then at 425°F for 5–10 minutes until crisp.
4. Salt immediately out of the oven.
5. Blend the sauce ingredients until smooth and stir in half the chives.
6. Toss the potatoes with the sauce, top with the remaining chives and parmesan, and serve immediately.
