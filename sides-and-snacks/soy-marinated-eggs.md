# Soy-Marinated Eggs

> **Stub.** Not written or tested yet.

## Starting notes

- Soft-boiled eggs in a soy, honey and aromatics marinade, served over rice.
- Instagram reference: Marinated eggs by @katgao (<https://www.instagram.com/reel/DUCe8mmEQcS/>). Same dish, different build — 2 tbsp dark soy + 1/2 cup light soy, 1/2 cup chopped onion, 1/4 cup honey, 1 tbsp sesame oil, 1–2 tbsp each of green onion, garlic and dill, optional MSG, chili flakes and sesame seeds; 6 minute eggs, 10 minute ice bath, minimum 3 hours in the fridge. The dill is the unusual note worth testing.
- The marinade is the same family as [`sauces/chicken/soy-ginger-glaze.md`](../sauces/chicken/soy-ginger-glaze.md) — worth comparing once both are written.
- Ledger entry: [`prospects/instagram.md`](../prospects/instagram.md) — entry 3. @katgao's version is entry 4.

## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient | Notes |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 6–8 | Eggs | boiled |
| 1/2 cup | Soy sauce |  |
| 1/2 cup | Chicken broth or water |  |
| ~1/4 cup | Honey |  |
| 1/4–1/2 | Onion | medium, diced |
| 1 | Green onion | diced |
| 3 | Garlic cloves | minced |
| 1 | Jalapeño | diced |
| 1 tbsp | Sesame seeds | toasted |
|  | Sesame oil and furikake | to drizzle at the end |

## Method

1. Cut and combine all the marinade ingredients in a mixing bowl.
2. Boil the eggs to your liking — 7–8 minutes in the video — and drop straight into an ice bath.
3. Peel and marinate.
4. Serve over rice, drizzled with sesame oil and furikake.
