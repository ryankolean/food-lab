## Method

1. Blanch the peeled garlic cloves in salted water, then drain.
2. Simmer the garlic with the bay leaf in milk and cream until completely soft.
3. Strain, keeping the cooking liquid.
4. Mash the softened garlic with butter, then whisk the liquid back in until smooth and pourable.
5. Season and pour over roast chicken.
