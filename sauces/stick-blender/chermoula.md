# Chermoula

The North African herb, garlic and spice sauce used as both a marinade and a finishing sauce, especially for
fish. This is Marcus Samuelsson's version from *The Soul of a New Cuisine*, his book on African cooking,
reprinted in a 2014 NPR piece alongside two charmoula recipes from Paula Wolfert's *The Food of Morocco*.

## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient | Notes |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| ~40 g | Garlic | 8 cloves, peeled |
| ~15 g | Flat-leaf parsley | ½ cup small sprigs |
| ~10 g | Cilantro | ⅓ cup small sprigs |
| 2 | Lemons, zest only | ~4 g, grated. Use a Microplane |
| ~9 g | Paprika | 4 teaspoons |
| ~5 g | Chili powder | 2 teaspoons |
| ~4 g | Ground cumin | 2 teaspoons |
| ~215 g | Olive oil | 1 cup |

## Method

1. Put the garlic, parsley, cilantro, lemon zest, paprika, chili powder, cumin and about ¼ cup of the oil in a
   quart deli container. Blend on low with the stick blender, head on the bottom, pulsing and moving the head
   around, to a **coarse** purée, not smooth.
2. With the blender running, add the rest of the oil in a thin, steady stream until it's a thick paste.

## To serve

As a marinade for fish or shrimp (an hour or so), or spooned over grilled fish, chicken, eggplant or roast
vegetables.

## Storage

- **Fridge:** Up to 2 weeks in a tightly covered container (per source). Herbs will darken.
- **Freezer:** Untested. Herb-and-oil sauces usually freeze well in small portions. Test using the
  [freezer test protocol](../README.md#test-protocol).

## Notes

### From the source
- Blend the garlic, herbs, zest and spices on low speed to a **coarse** purée, then stream in the oil with
  the blender running.
- There's no salt in Samuelsson's recipe.
- **Paula Wolfert, in the same NPR piece** (quoting *The Food of Morocco*): "There is no one recipe for
  charmoula." It changes by region; she writes that in Marrakech a cook might add ginger, and names
  versions from Agadir (creamed onions), Tetouan (hot red pepper oil) and Tangier (thyme).
- Wolfert's eggplant charmoula in the piece is a much smaller batch: 1 garlic clove, 1 teaspoon sweet
  paprika, a pinch of hot paprika, ¾ teaspoon ground cumin, 3 tablespoons each cilantro and parsley,
  3 tablespoons lemon juice and ½ cup olive oil.

### Added later (not in the source, confirm or edit)
- **Stick-blender adaptation.** The source blends everything except the oil first; a stick blender needs some
  liquid to catch the garlic and herbs, so a quarter of the oil goes in early.
- Salt and a squeeze of lemon juice are optional additions to taste, not part of Samuelsson's recipe.
- **Gram estimates** are approximate.
