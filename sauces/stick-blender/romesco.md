# Romesco

The Catalan red pepper and nut sauce served with grilled calçots, fish and vegetables. José Andrés, who
introduced a generation of Americans to Spanish food through Jaleo and his books, makes his version with
roasted tomatoes, onion, garlic and bell pepper, dried ñora peppers, almonds and fried bread.

## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient | Notes |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| ~110 g | Spanish extra-virgin olive oil | ½ cup total: 1 tablespoon to coat the vegetables, 7 tablespoons for the sauce |
| 1 | Red bell pepper | Whole; ~200 g |
| 6 | Ripe plum tomatoes | Whole; ~500 g |
| 1 head | Garlic | Halved crosswise, papery outer skin removed |
| 1 | Spanish onion | Halved, unpeeled; ~250 g |
| 3 | Dried ñora peppers | Stemmed and seeded. Or dried ancho or another sweet dried chile |
| ~35 g | Blanched almonds | ¼ cup. Marcona are good here |
| ~28 g | White bread | About 1 oz, crust removed |
| ~15 g | Sherry vinegar | 1 tablespoon. Red wine vinegar works |
| ~2.5 g | Pimentón (sweet smoked paprika) | 1 teaspoon |
| ~6 g | Salt | About ½ tablespoon, to taste |

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Rub the pepper, tomatoes, garlic and onion with the tablespoon of oil and
   roast until very soft and browned: about 25 minutes for the tomatoes and garlic, up to an hour for the
   pepper and onion. Take each out as it's done.
2. While they roast, cover the ñoras with hot water and soak until soft, at least 30 minutes.
3. Toast the almonds in a little of the oil in a skillet over medium heat, about 1 minute, until golden. Fry
   the bread in the same pan about 30 seconds per side. Let both cool.
4. **Prep the nuts and bread for the stick blender.** Put the almonds in a zip-top bag and crush them small
   with a rolling pin or pan. Tear the bread into pieces and soak it in the vinegar plus a spoonful of the
   ñora water.
5. Peel the pepper (discard the seeds), peel the onion, slip the skins off the tomatoes and squeeze the garlic
   out of its skins. Scrape the soft flesh out of the ñoras (or use them whole if they're soft).
6. Put the tomatoes and pepper in a quart deli container first, then the onion, garlic, ñora, soaked bread,
   crushed almonds, pimentón and salt. Blend with the stick blender, head on the bottom, pulsing at first,
   then running steadily and moving it up and down. **Blend for 2–3 minutes**, resting the motor if it gets
   hot.
7. With the blender running, add the remaining oil in a stream. Taste for salt and vinegar. It should be
   thick, slightly grainy and brick red.

## To serve

With grilled leeks or spring onions, grilled fish, potatoes or eggs. It's also a good steak sauce.

## Storage

- **Fridge:** About 5 days (estimate). Bring to room temperature before serving.
- **Freezer:** Untested. Nut and bread sauces usually freeze well; stir or re-blend after thawing. Test using
  the [freezer test protocol](../README.md#test-protocol).

## Notes

### From the source
- Traditionally romesco uses only dried ñora peppers; Andrés adds the fresh roasted bell pepper.
- The bread is there to thicken the sauce.
- If you can't find ñoras, use any sweet, slightly smoky dried pepper.

### Added later (not in the source, confirm or edit)
- **Nuts are the hard part for a stick blender.** It can't grind whole almonds the way a food processor does.
  Crushing toasted almonds small first, soaking the bread, and blending for a long time gets a good, slightly
  rustic texture. **Fallback:** swap the almonds for the same weight of almond flour, toasted in the dry pan
  until golden. Expect some grit either way; romesco is meant to be a little coarse.
- **Fit check.** Roasted and peeled, the vegetables shrink a lot, but if everything fills the deli container
  past about three-quarters, blend in two batches and stir them together.
- **Oven temperature and roasting times** are estimates; the summaries found didn't include them.
- Hazelnuts are common in other romesco recipes but not in Andrés's.
- **Gram estimates** for the vegetables are approximate. The source page couldn't be opened from this
  session; quantities came via search.
