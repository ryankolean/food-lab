# Basic Tahini Sauce (Zahav)

The tahini sauce that the rest of the *Zahav* cookbook is built on. Garlic is steeped in lemon juice and
strained out, so the sauce gets garlic flavor without raw garlic bite, then tahini is whipped with ice water
until it turns pale and fluffy. Michael Solomonov's Zahav in Philadelphia won the James Beard Award for
Outstanding Restaurant in 2019.

## Ingredients

Half of the source recipe, so it fits a quart deli container with room to blend.

| Amount | Ingredient | Notes |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| ½ head | Garlic | Unpeeled cloves; ~25 g. Source: 1 head |
| ~90 g | Lemon juice | ~6 tablespoons (2–3 lemons). Source: ¾ cup |
| ~3 g | Kosher salt | ¾ teaspoon, split between the two steps. Source: ~1½ teaspoons |
| ~250 g | Tahini | 1 generous cup, stirred well. Source: 2 generous cups |
| ~0.5 g | Ground cumin | ¼ teaspoon. Source: ½ teaspoon |
| ~180 g | Ice water | About ¾ cup, added gradually. Source: ~1½ cups |

## Method

1. Break the garlic into cloves (no need to peel). Put them in a quart deli container with the lemon juice
   and a pinch of the salt. Tilt the container so the liquid pools and blend with the stick blender for a
   few seconds to a coarse purée.
2. **Let it steep 10 minutes.**
3. Strain the lemon juice through a fine sieve into a bowl, pressing on the solids. Discard the solids and
   rinse the container.
4. Pour the strained lemon juice back into the container. Add the tahini, cumin and remaining salt. Blend;
   it will seize and go thick.
5. With the blender running, add ice water a couple of tablespoons at a time, moving the head up and down,
   until the sauce is smooth, pale and thick but spoonable. Taste and adjust salt, lemon or cumin.

## To serve

Spooned over roast vegetables, grilled meat or falafel, or as the base for hummus. Thin with more ice water
for a drizzling sauce.

## Storage

- **Fridge:** About 1 week (per source). It thickens in the fridge; whisk in a little ice water to loosen.
- **Freezer:** Untested. One reprint of the source says it freezes for up to a month. Test using the
  [freezer test protocol](../README.md#test-protocol).

## Notes

### From the source
- **The 10-minute steep is the key step.** It pulls the garlic flavor into the lemon juice.
- Add the ice water a few tablespoons at a time. The tahini seizes before it loosens; keep going.
- Whisk in a few tablespoons of ice water to loosen before refrigerating.

### Added later (not in the source, confirm or edit)
- **Halved** so the finished sauce fits a quart deli container with room to blend. The full batch is about
  a quart of sauce.
- **Stick-blender adaptation.** The source uses a food processor for the garlic and whisks the tahini by hand.
  A stick blender does both; it also whips the tahini fluffier.
- Reprints vary on salt and water (one Saveur version attributed to Zahav uses only 6 tablespoons of water,
  which looks like a different recipe). Quantities above follow the most consistent version.
- **Gram estimates** are approximate. The source page couldn't be opened from this session; quantities came
  via search.
