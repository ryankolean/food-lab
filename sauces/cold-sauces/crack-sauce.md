## Starting notes

- A peanut-lime dressing that coats a cold rice noodle salad. In the post it is tossed straight through the noodles and vegetables rather than spooned over.
- **No quantities anywhere.** The recipe is app-gated; the ingredients below are read off the on-screen labels and the order they went into the bowl.
- The whole dish is billed at 619 calories and 24.3 g protein across 4 servings, which is a useful check on how much peanut butter is actually in it — probably less than it looks.
- Watch the consistency: it goes in thick, then loosens visibly as the lime juice goes in. Water or warm water is likely doing some of that work off camera.
- Served with crispy baked tofu — crumbled, tossed in cornflour and a thick chilli sauce, sprayed with oil and baked — plus edamame, red pepper, cucumber, coriander, peanuts and spring onion.

## Method

1. Put the peanut butter, soy sauce and chilli paste in a bowl.
2. Add the lime juice and whisk until smooth and pourable.
3. Toss through cooked rice noodles with edamame, sliced red pepper, cucumber and coriander.
4. Top with the crispy tofu, peanuts and spring onion.
