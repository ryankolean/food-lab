# Marry Me Chicken Salad Dressing

> **Stub.** Not written or tested yet.

## Starting notes

- The flavours of marry me chicken — sun-dried tomato, garlic, Calabrian chili, basil — turned into a dressing for chicken salad.
- Full quantities were given in the caption. Makes 6 servings; keeps 3 days in the fridge.
- If it splits, the fix given is to whisk dollops of it into an egg yolk plus 1/2 tsp mustard.
- Salad components are listed under Notes rather than in the table, since the dressing is the part worth keeping.
- Ledger entry: [`prospects/instagram.md`](../../prospects/instagram.md) — entry 35.

## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient | Notes |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 2 tbsp | Extra-virgin olive oil |  |
| 2 tbsp | Sun-dried tomato oil | from the jar |
| 3 | Garlic cloves |  |
| 4–6 halves | Sun-dried tomatoes | to taste |
| 1 tbsp | Tomato paste |  |
| 1 tsp | Oregano |  |
| 1 tsp | Mustard |  |
| 3 tbsp | Mayonnaise |  |
| 3 | Calabrian chilis | or 1/2 tsp red chili flakes; optional |
|  | Basil stems | a few, optional |
|  | Salt | if needed |

## Method

1. Add all the dressing ingredients to a blender or small food processor.
2. Blend for a minute or two, until smooth. Taste and salt if needed.
3. If it does not emulsify, put an egg yolk and 1/2 tsp mustard in a separate bowl and whisk small dollops of the dressing into it.
4. Toss with the salad: 2–3 cooked and diced chicken breasts, 4 sun-dried tomato halves thinly sliced, 1 small shallot or 1/2 red onion finely chopped, 4 chopped celery sticks and 2 handfuls of sliced basil.
