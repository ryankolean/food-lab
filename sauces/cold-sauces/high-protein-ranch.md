# High-Protein Ranch

> **Stub.** Not written or tested yet.

## Starting notes

- Cottage cheese and Greek yogurt standing in for the mayo and buttermilk in a normal ranch.
- Claimed per 2 tbsp serving: 38 kcal, 2 g fat, 5 g protein — roughly 4x fewer calories, 8x less fat and 3x the protein of standard ranch.
- No quantities were given; the ratio of cottage cheese to yogurt is the thing to work out.
- Rests overnight before serving — the seasoning needs that time to bloom.
- Ledger entry: [`prospects/instagram.md`](../../prospects/instagram.md) — entry 22.

## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient | Notes |
| ---: | --- | --- |
|  | Ranch seasoning mix | neutral / unsweetened |
|  | Cottage cheese | blended smooth first |
|  | Greek yogurt | 2% |
|  | MSG |  |
|  | White vinegar |  |
|  | Milk | a splash, to loosen |

## Method

1. Blend the ranch seasoning into the cottage cheese until completely smooth.
2. Add the Greek yogurt, MSG and white vinegar and blend again.
3. Loosen with a splash of milk to pouring consistency.
4. Rest overnight in the fridge before serving.
