# Miso Chili Carbonara

> **Stub.** Not written or tested yet.

## Starting notes

- Dairy-free carbonara: white miso for umami, chili garlic oil for heat, no cheese.
- Worth trying for the technique as much as the flavour — the miso is loosened with starchy pasta water before it goes into the eggs, and the pasta is added *after* the sauce thickens.
- Egg emulsion, so this will not freeze. The miso-into-starchy-water step is the part that may transfer to the compound-butter workstream.
- Ledger entry: [`prospects/instagram.md`](../../prospects/instagram.md) — entry 28.

## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient | Notes |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 8 oz | Gluten free spaghetti | creator used GF; any spaghetti works |
| 2 tbsp | Kosher salt | for the pasta water |
| 3 + 3 | Eggs and egg yolks | room temperature |
| 1/2 tsp | Black pepper | ground |
| 2 tbsp | White miso paste |  |
| 2 tbsp | Olive oil |  |
| 1 tsp | Chili garlic oil | plus more to garnish |
|  | Fresh parsley | to garnish |

## Method

1. Whisk eggs, yolks and pepper together. Set aside to come to room temperature.
2. Cook the pasta two minutes shy of al dente. Reserve 2 cups of pasta water.
3. Loosen the miso with a generous splash of pasta water, then whisk it into the eggs.
4. Warm the olive oil, chili oil and a splash of pasta water in a skillet over low heat.
5. Temper the egg mixture with 1/2 cup hot pasta water.
6. Add the eggs to the warm (not hot) skillet slowly, whisking continuously, about ten minutes, until creamy.
7. Add the cooked pasta and cook on low until the sauce clings.
8. Garnish with chili oil and parsley.
