# Ninfa's Green Sauce (Houston Green Salsa)

Mama Ninfa Laurenzo's creamy green sauce from **Ninfa's on Navigation** in Houston, the restaurant credited
with popularizing fajitas. According to Houston regulars, almost every creamy green table salsa in the city
is a version of this one. Green tomatoes and tomatillos are boiled with garlic and jalapeño, then blended with
avocado and sour cream. It's tangy, rich and mild.

## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient | Notes |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 3 medium | Green (unripe) tomatoes | Rough-cut; ~450 g |
| 4 | Tomatillos | Husked and rinsed; ~200 g |
| 3 cloves | Garlic | Peeled; ~15 g |
| 1–2 | Jalapeños | Stemmed; ~25 g each |
| 3 medium | Ripe avocados | Pitted and scooped; ~450 g flesh |
| 2–3 sprigs | Cilantro | ~3 g |
| ~360 g | Sour cream | 1½ cups. Some versions use ¾ cup; see Notes |
| ~5 g | Salt | To taste |

## Method

1. Put the green tomatoes, tomatillos, garlic and jalapeños in a saucepan, barely cover with water and boil
   for about 15 minutes until soft. Drain and let cool slightly.
2. Put the cooked vegetables, avocados and cilantro in a tall container and blend smooth with the stick
   blender.
3. Blend in the sour cream and salt. Chill before serving.

## To serve

Cold, with chips. At Ninfa's it's served with fajitas, next to a red table salsa.

## Storage

- **Fridge:** 1–2 days with plastic wrap pressed on the surface.
- **Freezer:** Not recommended. Avocado and sour cream.

## Notes

### From the source
- The Homesick Texan version is adapted from a recipe published in the *Houston Chronicle*.

### Added later (not in the source, confirm or edit)
- **Quantities** come from published copies (Copykat, Good-Cooking, Anita's Table Talk), which agree with
  each other. Fain's own page couldn't be opened from this session. Sour cream ranges from ¾ to 1½ cups;
  start with ¾ cup and add more to taste.
- **Green tomatoes** can be hard to find. If you can't get them, more tomatillos are the usual substitute.
- This is the mildest salsa in the collection.
