# Ninfa's Green Sauce (Houston Green Salsa)

The creamy green sauce credited to Ninfa Laurenzo, the late owner of the Houston chain **Ninfa's**. Lisa Fain
calls it "ubiquitous all over town": it's rare to find a Houston Mexican restaurant without it. Green tomatoes
and tomatillos are simmered with garlic and jalapeño in their own juice, blended with avocado, then finished
with sour cream. It's tangy, rich and mild.

## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient | Notes |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 3 medium | Green (unripe) tomatoes | Chopped coarsely; ~450 g |
| 4 | Tomatillos | Husked and chopped; ~200 g |
| 1–2 | Jalapeños | Stemmed and chopped coarsely; ~25 g each |
| 3 small cloves | Garlic | ~10 g |
| 3 medium | Ripe avocados | Peeled, pitted and sliced; ~450 g flesh |
| 4 sprigs | Cilantro | ~5 g |
| 1 teaspoon | Kosher salt | |
| ~360 g | Sour cream | 1½ cups |

## Method

1. Put the green tomatoes, tomatillos, jalapeños and garlic in a saucepan. No water: the tomatoes supply the
   liquid. Bring to a boil, then lower the heat and simmer 10–15 minutes until soft.
2. Take it off the heat and let it cool slightly.
3. Put the cooked vegetables and their juices, the avocados, cilantro and salt in a quart deli container and
   blend smooth with the stick blender.
4. Stir in the sour cream. Chill before serving.

## To serve

Cold, with chips, tacos or enchiladas.

## Storage

- **Fridge:** 1–2 days with plastic wrap pressed on the surface (estimate; not stated in the source).
- **Freezer:** Not recommended. Avocado and sour cream.

## Notes

### From the source
- The Homesick Texan version is adapted from the *Houston Chronicle*'s published recipe, which the paper
  called its most-requested. The Chronicle's version used imitation sour cream; Fain uses real sour cream.
- **No green tomatoes?** Use 6 more tomatillos instead.
- The sour cream is stirred in by hand after blending, not blended.

### Added later (not in the source, confirm or edit)
- **Gram estimates** for the vegetables, cilantro and sour cream are approximate.
- This is the mildest salsa in the collection.
