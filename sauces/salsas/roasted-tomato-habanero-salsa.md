# Roasted Tomato-Habanero Salsa (Chiltomate)

Also called *chiltomate*: broiled tomatoes, a broiled habanero for its fruity heat, and blackened onion. It's
very simple and tastes strongly of char.

## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient | Notes |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| ~454 g | Ripe tomatoes | 1 lb: 2 medium-large round or 6–8 plum |
| 1–2 | Fresh habanero chiles | Stemmed; ~8 g each. 1 for medium-hot, 2 for hot |
| 1 small | Onion | Sliced ½ in thick; ~100 g |
| ~8 g | Cilantro | About ½ cup loosely packed, chopped |
| ~5 g | Salt | About 1 teaspoon |
| 1–2 tablespoons | Water | Only if too thick |

## Method

1. Heat the broiler with the rack 4 in below it. Put the whole tomatoes, habanero(s) and sliced onion on a
   rimmed baking sheet. Broil until blackened and blistered, 5–6 minutes.
2. Take the onion and habaneros off. Flip the tomatoes and broil the other side until blackened and soft,
   5–6 minutes more. Let everything cool until you can handle it. Peel off the blackened tomato skins if you
   wish.
3. *(Stick-blender version; see Notes.)* Put the tomatoes and their juices, the habanero(s), the roasted
   onion, the cilantro and the salt into a quart deli container. Blend with the stick blender.
4. If it's too thick to spoon, stir in a tablespoon or two of water. Taste for salt.

## To serve

At room temperature. The classic pairing is cochinita pibil or poc chuc, and it's also very good on eggs.

## Storage

- **Fridge:** About 5 days (estimate; not stated in the source).
- **Freezer:** Untested. A good candidate.

## Notes

### From the source
- Bayless's original is a **very coarse** salsa, neither chunky nor smooth: the habaneros are roughly chopped,
  then pulsed with the tomatoes in a blender or food processor. The broiled onion is chopped into small bits
  and stirred in with the cilantro and salt.

### Added later (not in the source, confirm or edit)
- **Stick-blender adaptation:** to fit the no-knife, smooth brief, the roasted onion and cilantro are blended
  in rather than chopped and stirred in. That makes it smoother and slightly sweeter. Pulse briefly for
  something closer to the original. Try both.
- **Habanero handling:** wear gloves when stemming roasted habaneros. Start with one and taste before adding
  the second.
- **Gram estimates** for the tomatoes, habaneros, onion, cilantro and salt are approximate, not from the
  source.
