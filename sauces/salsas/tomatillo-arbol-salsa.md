# Tomatillo-Árbol Chile Salsa

Bayless's fiery red-brown salsa: about sixteen toasted árbol chiles, six large cloves of roasted garlic and a
pound of broiled tomatillos. It's very spicy, very garlicky and tart.

## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient | Notes |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| ~14 g | Dried chiles de árbol | ½ oz (about 16), stemmed |
| 6 large cloves | Garlic | Unpeeled; ~30 g |
| ~454 g | Tomatillos | 1 lb (10–12 medium), husked and rinsed |
| ~60 g | Water | About ¼ cup, to thin |
| ~5 g | Salt | Usually a scant teaspoon |
| ½ teaspoon | Sugar | Optional; ~2 g |

## Method

1. In an ungreased skillet over medium heat, toast the chiles, stirring, until very aromatic, about 1 minute.
   Cover them with hot tap water and let them rehydrate 30 minutes.
2. In the same skillet over medium heat, roast the unpeeled garlic, turning regularly, until soft and dark in
   spots, about 15 minutes. Cool and peel.
3. Heat the broiler. Put the tomatillos on a baking sheet about 4 in below the very hot broiler until darkly
   roasted and blackened in spots, about 5 minutes. Flip and roast the other side 4–5 minutes more. Cool, and
   keep all the juices.
4. Drain the chiles. Put the tomatillos and their juices, the chiles and the garlic in a quart deli container and
   blend smooth with the stick blender.
5. Stir in enough water to make it spoonable, usually about ¼ cup. Season with salt and the sugar if you're
   using it (it softens the tomatillo tartness).

## To serve

With tacos, carne asada or chips. It's very hot, so use it sparingly.

## Storage

- **Fridge:** Several days (per source).
- **Freezer:** Untested. A good candidate.

## Notes

### From the source
- The sugar is optional and only there to balance tart tomatillos.

### Added later (not in the source, confirm or edit)
- **Stick blender:** the 30-minute soak in the source already makes this work. Blend for a full minute or
  more to break down the chile skins.
- **Gram estimates** for the garlic, tomatillos, water, salt and sugar are approximate, not from the source.
